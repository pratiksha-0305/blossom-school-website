Why Blossom?
The name Blossom symbolizes growth, learning, and new beginnings. Just as a flower blossoms with proper care and nourishment, every child has the potential to grow into a confident, knowledgeable, and responsible individual. At Blossom School, we believe in nurturing each student's talents, encouraging curiosity, and helping them achieve their fullest potential in a safe and inspiring learning environment.

>

Our History

Yashwant Shikshan Sanstha’s Blossom English School, Mayani was established in 2009 with a noble vision of providing quality English education to every child. The school was founded by Late Shri Shankar (Abba) Shivaji Mali, whose dream was to ensure that children from all backgrounds, especially those whose parents could not afford high educational expenses, had the opportunity to learn English and build a brighter future.

About Our School

Spread across a 2-acre campus, Blossom English School offers a peaceful and student-friendly environment for learning. The school has a large playground that encourages sports, physical fitness, teamwork, and the overall development of every child. We believe that education extends beyond the classroom, helping students grow academically, socially, emotionally, and physically.

At Blossom English School, we are committed to nurturing confident, disciplined, and responsible individuals through quality education, modern teaching methods, and strong moral values. Our teachers work with dedication to create an atmosphere where every child feels valued, encouraged, and inspired to achieve their full potential.
